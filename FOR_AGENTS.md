The canonical example is the pyramids, anchored first on Khufu's Great Pyramid. The research target is the material history connecting physical possibility, human operations, preservation and surviving evidence.

Read `corpus/atlas.md`, `corpus/atlas.json`, `REUSE.md` and `UI-DECOMPOSITION.md`. The existing twelve-coordinate kernel and common-algebra contracts are the substrate. Reuse the declared owners and mechanisms. Do not create a new Sourcery lane or registry as a substitute for discovering an existing owner.

Keep mathematical compatibility, numerical results, measured material response and historical attribution separate. A list of ingredients is not a reaction history. A modern experiment is not evidence that an ancient process occurred. A source reference is not independent confirmation. Missing or erased traces require a preservation and detection model.

Preserve the nine operation families, seventeen stations, twenty-six candidate bridges, joint inputs, negative constraints and explicit residuals. Extend them with exact evidence and scoped status. Never silently flatten a multi-input relation into independent binary edges.

Preserve joint connection inputs, ordered route direction, explicit residuals and scoped terminal dispositions. New candidate questions require rivals, defeat conditions and prior-art boundaries.
