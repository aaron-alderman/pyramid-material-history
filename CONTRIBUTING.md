# Contribute a bridge or a clean negative result

Start with a named object, component, sample, process or detector record. Identify its monument, context, material and timescale. Choose one of the nine operation families for each atomic output.

For a proposed bridge, supply source and target types, the connecting map or mechanism, assumptions, units and normalization, preserved quantities, information losses, evidence and a distinguishing test. Retain all required joint inputs. Preserve competing histories until a justified comparison separates them.

A useful return packet contains:

1. The exact question and source record IDs.
2. Preparation or observation context and source identities.
3. The calculation, experiment or argument and its assumptions.
4. Raw or reproducible derived records and uncertainty.
5. A comparator and a condition that would defeat the proposal.
6. What follows, what remains unresolved and the next useful measurement.

Edit research in `corpus/`. Edit presentation declarations in `data/publication.json`. Keep vendored mechanisms exact; a change to an existing mechanism needs its own source review and updated reuse identity. Do not add a parallel scientific kernel or a bespoke filter rule in browser code.

Run `node scripts/build.mjs` and `node --test scripts/qualification.test.mjs` before proposing a change. A passing software check establishes its stated scope, not historical occurrence or new physics.
